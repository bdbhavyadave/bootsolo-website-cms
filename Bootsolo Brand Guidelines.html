01 — Brand essence

The team that ropes you in

Bootsolo picks up the marketing work where a one-person team's time and budget run out. We're young, AI-ready, and ROI-obsessed — a mix of fresh graduates and experienced leaders who've climbed the mountain before.

What we stand for

The climb
A solo ascent of a snowy summit at sunrise, with a tracked route to the top. It drives the logo, the palette, and the product language.
P&L positive
Every recommendation ties to a founder's bottom line. ROI-focused, never vanity metrics.
Backpack budget
Priced for a backpack, not a boardroom. Built for founders doing it solo on limited cash.
Optimistic
Encouraging, founder-to-founder. Warm and direct — a guide rooting for you, never corporate.

Product language follows the climb

Pricing tiers and product surfaces lean into the metaphor — used for rhythm, never forced: Basecamp (free) → Climber (popular middle) → Summit.

02 — Voice & tone

Encouraging, founder-to-founder, still direct

One founder talking to another, not an agency addressing a market. Warm, plain-spoken, confident without bragging. Always “you” (the reader) and “we” (us).

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere — headlines, buttons, nav, titles. The only uppercase is the tracked label/eyebrow (0.10em).
  • Outcome-first, but human. Lead with the win and what it means for a solo founder.
  • A little climb metaphor goes a long way — use it for rhythm, don't overdo it.
  • Never emoji. Energy comes from color, the illustration, and the writing.
  • Banned words: leverage, synergize, ecosystem, cutting-edge, solutions, empower, unlock, seamless, robust, best-in-class, revolutionary, game-changing.

Do & don't

Do
  • Keep clear space of at least the sun's diameter around the mark.
  • Use the navy tile on photos or busy backgrounds.
  • Pair the wordmark in DM Sans 600, lowercase.
Don't
  • Recolor the peak away from Sunrise orange.
  • Add gradients, shadows, or outlines to the mark.
  • Stretch, rotate, or Title-Case the wordmark.

Light + dark

The site is light by default. Add class="dark" (or .theme-dark) to flip the semantic variables for navy bands. The dashboard runs fully dark. Semantic roles — --bg, --bg-elevated, --fg1/2/3, --border, --brand, --accent — resolve automatically in each theme.

08 — Elevation & motion

Soft lift, micro-motion

Resting cards get a faint shadow on light; hover lifts to a larger shadow with a 3px translate. The Sunrise glow marks the one featured element. Motion is hover-only — 170ms, cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1). No bounces, parallax, or ambient loops.

09 — Iconography

Lucide, stroke-only

Clean ~1.75px stroke, rounded joins, no fill. Icon color follows text; Sunrise or Ice is reserved for highlighted/feature icons. No emoji, no multicolor or filled packs.

Load: <script src="https://unpkg.com/lucide@latest"></script> then lucide.createIcons(). Unicode arrows (→) are fine inside buttons and links.

10 — Imagery

The tracked summit climb

No stock photos. The signature image is the summit illustration — a flat, geometric, color-blocked scene: navy dawn sky, a gold sun, ice-blue ranges, a snow-capped hero peak, and a dashed Sunrise route switchbacking to a tracker pin at the top.

assets/hero-summit.svg — build new illustrations in the same flat, geometric, sunrise-palette style. No gradients, no noise/grain.

11 — Buttons

Buttons

600 weight, sentence case, 44px default height, 11px radius. Primary carries the Sunrise glow. Hover lifts 1px and darkens to the −700 tone.

Inputs
