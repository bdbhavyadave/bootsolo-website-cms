Northwind: +312% signups in one quarter

At a glance: A one-person SaaS company went from founder-run, ad-hoc marketing to a running growth engine, and grew signups by 312% in a single quarter for less than the cost of a part-time hire.

The Problem

Sam was the founder, the engineer and the entire marketing department. Every hour spent on marketing was an hour away from the product, so marketing happened in bursts: a launch post here, a tweet thread there, then weeks of silence. Signups were flat, the product barely appeared when buyers asked search engines or AI assistants for tools in its category, and trial users who signed up were rarely nurtured into paying customers. Hiring a marketer wasn't affordable yet.

The Solution

Bootsolo became Northwind's marketing department. We combined three services into one system: Get found by AI to put Northwind into the answers buyers were already asking for, a Content engine to publish consistently without Sam writing every word, and Lifecycle automation to turn new signups into active users.

Our Approach & Milestones
Weeks 1–2
Map the route

Audited site, funnel and visibility across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Identified 25 core questions buyers ask before choosing tools in Northwind's category.

Weeks 2–6
Get found by AI

Rewrote key pages in answer-first structure, deployed schema graph data and direct comparison hubs, earning verified citations in AI answer engines.

Weeks 3–12
Run content engine

AI-assisted drafts refined by senior Bootsolo strategists published weekly. Sam's only operational commitment: one 20-minute review call weekly.

Weeks 5–12
Switch on lifecycle

Deployed behavioural onboarding triggers, nudges for stalled trial workspaces, and automated win-back workflows for inactive users.

At a glance: A bootstrapped D2C skincare brand cut its cost per sale by 58% by rebuilding its paid campaigns and automating the follow-up that ads alone couldn't do.

The Problem

Parallel was growing, but every sale was costing too much. Paid social was the main sales channel, and as ad costs rose the margins on a self-funded product shrank. Campaigns were managed in spare hours, creative was refreshed rarely, and shoppers who added to cart or visited twice were left to drift away. The brand needed more sales from the same budget, not a bigger budget.

The Solution

We restructured Parallel's paid Campaigns around the products and audiences that actually converted, and added Automation so every ad click had a follow-up path: abandoned cart, browse abandonment and post-purchase repeat flows across email and SMS.

Our Approach & Milestones
Phase 1
Audit the spend

Traced exact acquisition cost per order by product, audience and creative variant. Paused bleed campaigns burning cash without conversions.

Phase 2
Rebuild campaigns

Consolidated ad sets, anchored on hero skincare bundles, and launched an AI-assisted creative pipeline generating fresh visual hooks weekly.

Phase 3
Automate follow-up

Built abandoned-cart, browse-abandonment and replenishment notification journeys timed precisely to skincare jar usage cycles.

Phase 4
Optimise weekly

Shifted spend dynamically into top-converting ads weekly, paired with an executive 1-page financial scoreboard for founder clarity.

Case Study 05 Client: Cortex Writer & Founder: Priya N. Newsletter Services: Automation, Lifecycle
+89% Open Rate Lift

Cortex: A solo writer lifted open rates 89% with lifecycle automation

At a glance: A one-person newsletter turned a growing but disengaged list into an engaged readership by replacing one-size-fits-all sends with automated, behaviour-based journeys.

The Problem

Cortex's subscriber list was growing, but fewer readers were opening each issue. Every subscriber got the same emails no matter when they joined or what they cared about, new subscribers never got a proper welcome, and inactive readers dragged down deliverability. As a solo writer, the founder had time to write, not to manage segments and sequences.

The Solution

We set up Lifecycle journeys powered by Automation, so every reader got the right email at the right moment: a welcome series for new subscribers, interest-based segments, re-engagement for quiet readers, and list hygiene to protect inbox placement.

Our Approach & Milestones
Phase 1
Diagnose engagement

Inspected deliverability, sender reputation, and cohort drop-off rates across subscriber signup origins and subscription age.

Phase 2
Welcome properly

Engineered an automated 4-part welcome onboarding journey introducing evergreen best editions and surveying topical reader interests.

Phase 3
Segment & personalize

Implemented automated reader tagging by reading activity, incorporating AI subject line variation testing on each blast.

Phase 4
Re-engage or clean

Executed a 3-stage re-engagement trigger, automatically pruning cold subscribers to protect 99.2% primary inbox delivery.

“I just want to write. Bootsolo built the systems around my writing, and now more people actually open what I send.”
