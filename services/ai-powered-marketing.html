Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Straight answers about how we work, who owns what, and how quickly your pipeline activates.

Do I need to already use AI tools?
No. We set everything up and show you how it works. If you already pay for tools (like OpenAI, Make, Zapier, or Mailchimp), we'll wire those in first to keep your overhead minimal.
How soon will I see results?
Most clients have campaigns or funnels live within 3–4 weeks. Ad testing usually shows clear winners within the first month; automation results build as more leads enter your funnel.
Will AI-generated content sound robotic?
Not with us. AI produces the drafts and variations; a human strategist shapes the voice and approves what goes live. Your audience will never receive soulless corporate spam.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. All our monthly plans operate on month-to-month agreements with zero lock-in contracts. You can pause or cancel at any time with 7 days notice. All prompts, workflows, email templates, and audience datasets created during your engagement remain 100% yours forever.
Which tools do you work with?
Zapier, Make, n8n, major email and CRM platforms (HubSpot, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign, ConvertKit, Customer.io), Meta Ads, Google Ads, and LinkedIn Ads. If you use something else, ask us—we routinely build custom webhooks for niche tech stacks.
